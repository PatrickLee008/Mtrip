### Overview
This Product Requirements Document (PRD) outlines the mTrip Hotel Booking feature for the User App. The first release targets Myanmar travelers and hotel partners, ensuring a smooth and user-friendly booking process. Users can find hotels, choose rooms, apply discounts, and pay through various methods. Refunds for eligible cancellations will go to the mTrip Wallet. After their stay, users can manage their hotel reviews and ratings. This PRD details the user journey from hotel search to post-stay feedback.
### Problem
Travelers in Myanmar struggle to find a local hotel booking platform that provides genuine reviews, detailed hotel info, good prices, special deals, and a smooth booking process. This makes it hard for users to select hotels that fit their needs and budget, while many local hotels lack online presence and chances to reach travelers. These issues lower user trust, increase booking hassle, and present an opportunity for mTrip to offer a reliable hotel booking platform designed for the Myanmar market.
### Objectives
Deliver a seamless and intuitive hotel booking experience that enables users to search, compare, and book accommodations quickly and effortlessly.
Enhance booking confidence with AI-powered features by providing personalized hotel recommendations, AI-generated review summaries, and authentic verified guest reviews.
Build a trusted end-to-end travel platform by integrating value-added services such as travel insurance, secure payments, and a modern user experience that increases customer satisfaction, booking conversions, and user retention.
### Constraints
Limited MVP scope and timeline – The initial release focuses on core hotel booking capabilities, with advanced features and future ecosystem services planned for subsequent phases.
Dependency on third-party integrations – Features such as hotel inventory, pricing, payment gateways, travel insurance, and notifications rely on external partners and APIs, which may affect delivery timelines.
AI feature maturity and data availability – AI recommendations and review summaries depend on the availability and quality of hotel, review, and user data, and will be continuously improved as the platform grows.

Module 1.1 : Multi-Hotel Booking
Feature: Single Checkout → Separate Booking Transactions
#### Feature Description
The Multi-Hotel Booking feature allows users to book multiple hotels across different destinations or stay periods within a single checkout. Users complete one payment transaction, while the system creates individual hotel booking transactions for each selected hotel. Each booking is managed independently after payment, including booking confirmation, modification, cancellation, refund, settlement, and booking history.
Note: This feature is subject to technical feasibility assessment with the Engineering Team before development.
Functional Requirements for Multi Hotel Booking

Module 2.1 - Long-Stay Deals
Overview
The Long Stay Deals feature enables users to discover and book accommodations with exclusive discounts for extended stays. Unlike the standard hotel booking entry, long-stay is provided as a dedicated entry point on the mTrip Home page to increase visibility and promote long-stay campaigns.
The booking journey remains largely consistent with the existing hotel booking flow, allowing users to enjoy a familiar booking experience while automatically receiving eligible long-stay discounts based on their stay duration.

Objectives
Promote long-stay accommodations through a dedicated Home page entry.
Encourage longer bookings by displaying attractive discount tiers.
Reuse the existing hotel booking workflow to reduce complexity.
Automatically calculate and apply eligible long-stay discounts.
Support long-stay exclusive promotions and coupons.
## Entry Point of “Long Stay Deals” | “Long Stay”
A dedicated "Long Stay" icon button shall be displayed on the mTrip Home page as a standalone service, separate from the existing Hotel entry.
Selecting the Long Stay entry shall navigate users to the Long Stay Deals search experience.

#### Module 7 : Feature 3 Lite Mode (Global UI Experience)

Settlement Architecture Diagram (System Architecture)

High Level Description
After booking completion, the Settlement Engine applies promotion funding rules and allocates funds to the Platform Commission, Partner Commission Payable, and Merchant Settlement Payable accounts, while the Refund Engine handles cancellation and refund processing.
Settlement Decision Flow (Business Rules)

Financial Settlement Matrix

| Scenario | Customer Payment | Hotel Settlement | mTrip Commission | Customer Refund |
| Successful Hotel Booking | Hotel Booking Amount | Hotel Amount − Commission / (e.g., MMK 50,000 −10%) | Commission deducted based on merchant agreement (e.g., 10%) | Not Applicable |
| Customer Cancels (Free Cancellation) | Paid | No Settlement | No Commission | 100% Refund to mTrip Wallet |
| Customer Cancels (Partially Refundable) | Paid | Settled according to hotel cancellation policy | Commission applied according to merchant agreement | Partial Refund to mTrip Wallet |
| Customer Cancels (Non-refundable) | Paid | Settled according to hotel cancellation policy | Commission retained according to merchant agreement | No Refund |
| Hotel Cancels Booking | Paid | No Settlement | No Commission | 100% Refund to mTrip Wallet |
| Payment Failed | Payment Unsuccessful | No Settlement | No Commission | No Charge |

8.3 Refund Settlement Matrix

| Scenario | Refund Amount | Refund Method | Settlement Action |
| Customer Cancels (Free Cancellation) | 100% | mTrip Wallet | No settlement to hotel merchant |
| Customer Cancels (Partially Refundable) | According to hotel cancellation policy | mTrip Wallet | Settlement adjusted according to refundable amount |
| Customer Cancels (Non-refundable) | No Refund | Not Applicable | Hotel settlement proceeds according to merchant agreement |
| Hotel Cancels Booking | 100% | mTrip Wallet | Hotel settlement cancelled |
| Payment Failed | 100% (if payment captured) or No Charge | Original payment reversal or automatic void | No settlement |


### Business Rules
Customers make a single payment to mTrip for the hotel booking.
mTrip deducts the agreed commission before settling the remaining amount to the hotel merchant.
Merchant settlement is processed according to the configured settlement cycle (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly).
Hotel settlement is processed only for completed bookings after the guest has checked out.
Refund eligibility follows the hotel's cancellation policy.
All approved refunds are credited to the customer's mTrip Wallet.
Cancelled bookings that are fully refunded are excluded from merchant settlement.
Settlement reports shall be generated for Finance and hotel merchants for reconciliation purposes.
Each hotel merchant shall have an independently configurable settlement cycle managed through the Admin Portal.
The system shall support different settlement cycles for different hotel merchants (e.g., daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or specific settlement dates).
The settlement engine shall automatically process settlements according to each merchant's configured settlement schedule without affecting other merchants.
Settlement batches shall group eligible booking transactions based on each merchant's configured settlement cycle.
The system shall maintain accounting entries for every financial transaction, including booking payments, commissions, merchant payables, settlement payouts, refunds, adjustments, taxes, fees, and promotional transactions.
Promotion-related transactions shall record the promotion funding source (e.g., Merchant-funded, mTrip-funded, Shared-funded, or Partner-funded) to ensure accurate settlement and accounting.
When promotions are applied, the system shall automatically calculate and allocate promotional costs according to the configured funding responsibility before determining the merchant's final settlement amount.
Every financial transaction shall generate auditable accounting records to support reconciliation, financial reporting, and auditing.
The system shall maintain a complete audit trail of settlement calculations, adjustments, payout status, promotion funding, and accounting history for every booking transaction.
The settlement engine shall support multiple payable parties (e.g., Hotel Merchant, Agent, Affiliate, or External Partner) based on the configured commission and settlement rules.

## Module 10.1: Booking Risk Management & Appeal
User Fraud Management
To protect the mTrip platform from abuse while maintaining a fair user experience, the system shall implement a configurable multi-level fraud management mechanism based on user behavior, booking patterns, and cancellation history. Fraud detection rules are evaluated automatically using configurable thresholds managed through the mTrip Admin Portal, while the Operations or Risk Management team reviews flagged accounts and performs manual enforcement actions when necessary.
#### Level 1 – Warning
Users who exceed the configured fraud threshold will receive an in-app warning regarding unusual booking or cancellation activities. No feature restrictions are applied at this stage, and the user's activities continue to be monitored.
#### Level 2 – Account Suspension
If the user's fraud score reaches the next configured threshold, the system shall automatically apply an account suspension. Suspended users cannot use the app until the suspension is removed through a successful appeal or an administrative decision.
#### Appeal Process 
Users under account suspension  may submit an appeal through the application by providing:
## A written explanation.
## Supporting attachments (photo or file). [can attach screenshots or files (up to 20 MB).]
## The Operations or Risk Management team shall review the appeal together with the user's booking history and fraud indicators. Based on the review, administrators may:
## Remove the feature restriction.
## Maintain the restriction.
## Escalate the account to Banned status if fraudulent activity is confirmed.
## The user shall be notified of the appeal result within the application.
#### Banned Account
For confirmed or repeated fraudulent activities, administrators may permanently ban the user account. Once banned:
## An account suspension alert shall be displayed upon login.
## All application features shall be disabled.
## The user may only log out of the application.
#### Fraud Detection Rules
## The fraud detection engine shall support configurable rules and thresholds, including but not limited to:
## Excessive booking cancellations within a configurable period.
## Cancellation rate exceeding the configured threshold.
## Multiple bookings for the same hotel on the same stay dates exceeding the configured threshold.
## Additional fraud rules defined by the Operations or Risk Management team.
## The mTrip Admin Portal shall provide fraud management capabilities, including:
## Fraud threshold and rule configuration.
## Dashboard of flagged users.
## Fraud level (Warning, Feature Restriction, Banned).
## Fraud triggers history and reasons.
## Booking and cancellation history.
## Same hotel / same stay date booking detection.
## Appeal review queue with submitted explanations and attachments.
## Administrative actions to approve appeals, maintain restrictions, remove restrictions, suspend users, or ban users.

Review Rating Score Calculation
Each user rates the hotel using 5 rating categories, with each category scored from 1 to 5 points.

| Category | Score Range |
| Overall | 1–5 |
| Cleanliness | 1–5 |
| Location | 1–5 |
| Service | 1–5 |
| Amenities | 1–5 |
| Maximum Total | 25 |

##### Calculate Each User's Review Score
The system adds the user's scores from all 5 categories.
Formula: User Review Score = (Total Category Score ÷ 25) × 10
The result is converted from a 5-point category system to a 10-point review score.
Example:

| Category | User Score |
| Overall | 5 |
| Cleanliness | 4 |
| Location | 5 |
| Service | 4 |
| Amenities | 4 |
| Total | 22 / 25 |

Calculation: (22 ÷ 25) × 10 = 8.8
Therefore, the user's review score is 8.8 / 10.
##### 
##### 
##### 
##### 
##### Calculate the Hotel's Overall Rating
The hotel's overall rating is calculated using the average of all approved user review scores.
Formula: Hotel Overall Rating = Sum of all User Review Scores ÷ Number of Approved Reviews
Example:

| User | User Review Score |
| User 1 | 8.8 |
| User 2 | 9.2 |
| User 3 | 7.6 |
| User 4 | 8.4 |

Calculation: (8.8 + 9.2 + 7.6 + 8.4) ÷ 4 = 8.5
Therefore: Hotel Overall Rating = 8.5 / 10
The hotel rating is displayed to 1 decimal place.
#####  Review Eligibility for Hotel Rating
Only approved and published reviews are included in the hotel rating calculation.
Rejected, unpublished, or deleted reviews are not included.
If the hotel has no approved reviews, display “New · No reviews yet” instead of a numerical rating.
When the first approved review is published, the hotel rating is calculated using that review.
The hotel rating is recalculated whenever a new approved review is published or an existing approved review is removed.

17.4 Multi-Room Selection – Room Count Synchronization
The room count selected on the Hotel Search page is linked to the room quantity on the Room List / Room Selection page.
When the user selects a room type by clicking “Choose”, the room quantity button will automatically use the same room count requested in the Hotel Search. This avoids requiring the user to manually re-enter the number of rooms.

| Search Page Room Count | Room List Before Selection | User Action | After Clicking “Choose” |
| 1 Room | Choose | Click Choose | − 1 + |
| 2 Rooms | Choose | Click Choose | − 2 + |
| 3 Rooms | Choose | Click Choose | − 3 + |
| 4 Rooms | Choose | Click Choose | − 4 + |

Normal Single-Room Scenario
For a normal single-room booking:
User selects 1 Room on the Hotel Search page.
User searches for hotels and opens the Room Selection page.
Each available room type displays a “Choose” button.
User clicks “Choose” for the desired room type.
The “Choose” button changes to the room quantity control − 1 +.
The room quantity is initialized to 1, based on the room count selected on the Hotel Search page.
#### Multi-Room Scenario
For example, if the user searches with 2 Rooms, the selected room type will automatically start with 2 rooms after clicking “Choose”.

Key rule: The initial room quantity is always synchronized with the room count selected during Hotel Search. The user can then adjust the quantity using the − / + control, subject to the total room requirement.

Applicable to both Lite Mode and Full Mode: The same “Choose” → − N + interaction will be used in both modes to maintain consistency and avoid user confusion when selecting rooms.

17.5 Promotion Coupon Eligibility and Admin Configuration
Coupon eligibility is controlled by the Admin Portal. Admin can configure the conditions for each coupon based on the promotion requirement.
The system should check the booking against the configured conditions before applying the coupon.

| Coupon Condition | Admin Configuration Example | System Behavior |
| Room-Specific | Select specific room type(s) | Apply discount only to the selected room type(s). |
| Hotel-Specific | Select specific hotel(s) | Apply discount only to the selected hotel(s). |
| Multi-Room | Minimum room count = 2 | Coupon is eligible when the booking has 2 or more rooms. |
| Multi-Hotel | Minimum hotel count = 2 | Coupon is eligible when the booking contains 2 or more hotels. |
| Minimum Booking Amount | Minimum = 200,000 MMK | Coupon is eligible when the eligible booking amount reaches the configured amount. |
| Early Bird | Book at least 30 days before check-in | Coupon is eligible when the booking meets the configured advance-booking period. |
| Long Stay | Minimum stay = 5 nights | Coupon is eligible when the configured minimum stay is reached. |
| Specific Stay Date | Select specific date/period | Coupon is eligible only for bookings within the configured stay period. |
| Maximum Discount | Maximum = 50,000 MMK | Discount cannot exceed the configured maximum amount. |

#### Booking Type Examples
The same booking can satisfy multiple conditions at the same time.

| Booking Scenario | Possible Coupon Conditions |
| 1 Hotel + 1 Room | Room-Specific, Hotel-Specific, Minimum Booking Amount, Early Bird, etc. |
| 1 Hotel + Multiple Rooms | Room-Specific, Hotel-Specific, Multi-Room, Minimum Booking Amount, etc. |
| Multiple Hotels + 1 Room per Hotel | Hotel-Specific, Multi-Hotel, Minimum Booking Amount, Early Bird, etc. |
| Multiple Hotels + Multiple Rooms | Room-Specific, Hotel-Specific, Multi-Room, Multi-Hotel, and other configured conditions |

Important: The booking type itself does not automatically determine whether a coupon is eligible. Admin configuration determines the eligibility rules.
For example, Admin may configure:
Coupon A: Hotel A only
Coupon B: Deluxe Room only
Coupon C: Minimum 2 rooms
Coupon D: Minimum 2 hotels
Coupon E: Hotel A + minimum 2 rooms
Coupon F: Minimum 2 hotels + minimum booking amount 500,000 MMK
The system should evaluate all configured conditions and apply the coupon only when the booking satisfies the required conditions.

17.6 Coupon Allocation and Discount Distribution
When a coupon is eligible, the system should determine which room(s) or hotel(s) receive the discount based on the coupon configuration.
For multi-room or multi-hotel bookings, the coupon discount should not be applied to ineligible rooms or hotels.

| Coupon Scenario | Example Booking | Coupon Allocation Rule |
| Room-Specific Coupon | Hotel A: Deluxe × 2 + Suite × 1 | Apply the coupon only to the eligible Deluxe rooms. Suite does not receive the discount. |
| Hotel-Specific Coupon | Hotel A + Hotel B in one booking | Apply the coupon only to the eligible rooms under the selected hotel. Do not apply it to other hotels. |
| Multi-Room Coupon | 2 or more rooms in one hotel | If the minimum room count condition is met, apply the configured discount according to the coupon rule. |
| Multi-Hotel Coupon | Hotel A + Hotel B | If the minimum hotel count condition is met, apply the configured discount to the eligible booking amount. |
| Multi-Room + Room-Specific | Deluxe × 2 + Suite × 1 | Check both conditions. If eligible, apply the discount only to the selected room type(s). |
| Multi-Hotel + Hotel-Specific | Hotel A + Hotel B | Apply the discount only to the configured hotel. Other hotels are excluded. |
| Booking-Level Coupon | Multiple hotels and rooms | Apply the discount to the eligible booking amount based on the configured coupon conditions. |
| Maximum Discount | 10% discount, maximum 30,000 MMK | Calculate the discount first, then cap the discount at the configured maximum amount. |

#### Discount Allocation for Fixed-Amount Coupons
For a fixed-amount coupon that applies to multiple eligible rooms, the discount should be distributed proportionally based on the eligible room price.
Example:
Room A = 50,000 MMK
Room B = 150,000 MMK
Eligible total = 200,000 MMK
Coupon discount = 20,000 MMK

| Room | Room Price | Discount Allocation | Final Price |
| Room A | 50,000 MMK | 5,000 MMK | 45,000 MMK |
| Room B | 150,000 MMK | 15,000 MMK | 135,000 MMK |
| Total | 200,000 MMK | 20,000 MMK | 180,000 MMK |

Allocation Formula:
Room Discount = Total Coupon Discount × (Eligible Room Price ÷ Total Eligible Room Price)
This proportional allocation should be used when the coupon is configured to apply across multiple eligible rooms.
#### Important Rules
Coupon discount must only be applied to eligible rooms/hotels.
Ineligible rooms/hotels must not receive any part of the coupon discount.
For a fixed-amount coupon covering multiple eligible rooms, use proportional allocation based on eligible room price.
If the coupon has a maximum discount amount, the system must not exceed the configured limit.
The price breakdown should clearly show the coupon discount and the rooms/hotels to which it was applied.
The exact allocation method can be controlled by the Admin Portal where required.
